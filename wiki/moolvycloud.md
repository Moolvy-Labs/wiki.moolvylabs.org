# Welcome to Moolvy Cloud Infrastructure ☁️

**Moolvy Cloud** is a high-performance IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service) provider developed by MoolvyLabs. We provide unmanaged **VPS (Virtual Private Servers)** and scalable compute power for developers, devops engineers, and businesses. 

Our mission is to deliver reliable hardware, root access, and maximum network uptime, leaving full environment control in your hands.

---

## ⚠️ Infrastructure Support Policy (Unmanaged Service)

To maintain low costs and high performance, Moolvy Cloud operates strictly on an **unmanaged infrastructure model**:

* **Full Root Control:** We provide a clean OS installation. Configuration, firewalls, web servers, and software deployment are 100% the customer's responsibility.
* **No Managed Troubleshooting:** Technical support strictly covers hardware availability and network uptime. Support will not investigate internal OS errors, broken applications, or software issues.
* **Server Termination Policy:** If a server experiences unresolvable critical errors, network abuse, or compromise, technical support operations are strictly limited to **server deletion/termination**. Customers must maintain their own remote backups to prevent data loss.
